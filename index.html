Stories that hit home. And stay there.

We’re all about telling stories that make you think and stick with you long after the credits roll. Every frame, every scene, every character is crafted with intention and passion.

A film by Viktoriya Melnykova

The Choice

In the depths of her own home, an elderly woman confronts her greatest challenge when three scared-to-death Russian soldiers break in, seeking refuge from Ukrainian artillery fire. Trapped with the enemy, she devises a daring plan to outsmart and outlast them, proving that even in the face of adversity, humanity prevails.

Documentary

Life Train

The extraordinary journeys of courageous Ukrainian railway workers who risked their lives daily to evacuate people from the most dangerous war-torn regions. Featuring interviews with Boris Johnson, Andrii Shevchenko and more.

Award-winning script

Steph

After the enemy invades Ukraine, a seven-year-old aspiring artist must survive the violent occupation of her hometown, Mariupol, alongside her single mother. The script has won and been nominated for awards including LAFA, LICA, PIFA, Prisma and NYFA.

Drama series

A Promise to God

A journalist cuts ties with her unfaithful lover while expecting his child. Years later her son needs a donor, and the only hope is the cord blood of a sibling. A story about the fight for a little boy’s life that forces everyone to rethink what matters.
